Blitz Generals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Blitz Generals is a strategy game played against the computer or, online, against a friend, developed by ItzikGoldreer. It is free to play and is supported by ads. This policy explains what information the game handles, who handles it and what choices you have.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other personal details. The game has no user names, no passwords, no in-app purchases and no chat. Your settings and your last army are saved only on your own device.

When you play online (see below), the game uses a random player ID that is not linked to your name or to any of your accounts, and the online service keeps the record of a match while it is being played and for a while after it ends (see Keeping and deleting data below).

Online play

PLAY ONLINE lets you battle a friend who joins with an invite code. Online play runs on Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. When you open PLAY ONLINE for the first time, the game signs in anonymously with Unity's Authentication service, which creates a random player ID for your installation of the game. No name, email address or other account is needed or asked for.

To run an online match, Unity's servers process and store:

  • the anonymous player IDs of the two players and the invite code;
  • each player's army, the moves of the match and its result;
  • technical information needed to connect, such as your IP address and information about the device and the app.

This information is used only to run your online matches: to connect you with your friend, to check every move against the rules, and to let you return to a match after a lost connection. It is not used for advertising. Unity's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a full-screen ad between matches, and an optional rewarded ad that you can choose to watch in the army shop for extra points (games against the computer only). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects information and shares it with Google, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps;
  • crash and performance data from the ad software.

Depending on your choices and the law where you live, Google may use this information to show personalised or non-personalised ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised. In those regions you can change your choice at any time in the game under SETTINGS > AD CHOICES. The policy itself is always available in the game under SETTINGS > PRIVACY POLICY.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Online play is optional: if you never open PLAY ONLINE, no player ID is created and nothing about your matches leaves your device.

Crash and performance reports

The game is built with the Unity engine. To help fix crashes and performance problems, the Unity engine may automatically send crash reports and technical diagnostic data to Unity Technologies. This data describes the device and the error (for example the device model, operating system version and the error details) and may include a device identifier generated for the app. It does not include your name, contacts or files. Unity's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Security

The information sent by the ad, online and crash-report software travels over encrypted connections.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google for ads, Unity for online play and crash reports) for the purposes described in this policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device. The server deletes an invitation as soon as it is cancelled, and the record of a finished online match as soon as both players have left its result screen. Until then it is kept for at least ten minutes, so that a player who was away still sees the result and both can start a rematch; after that it is deleted the next time one of the players' games asks the server about it, or by an automatic clean-up that runs as new online games are created. A match that both players left unfinished is removed by the same clean-up once it has gone two hours without a move. So that each player holds only one online game at a time, the server also keeps a short note of the invite code of each player ID's latest game; the note is deleted together with that game. These records hold only the random player IDs, the invite code, the app version of each player's game, the armies and the moves.

How to delete your Blitz Generals data:

  • In the game: open PLAY ONLINE and tap DELETE MY ONLINE DATA at the bottom of the screen. Your anonymous online account is deleted from Unity's servers at once, together with the server's note of your latest game and the record of a finished match you had not yet left, and the game forgets your player ID on your device. An online match you are still in is ended (it counts as surrendered); its record, which your friend still sees, is deleted as described above. A new ID is created only if you play online again.
  • By email: send an email to itzik19912@gmail.com with the subject "Blitz Generals data deletion". If you have it, include the invite code of the online match (for example K7M-Q4X). We delete the online match records we can find for that request within 30 days and reply when it is done. The random player ID is not linked to your email or name, so we cannot find it from an email: to delete it, use DELETE MY ONLINE DATA in the game.

Information collected by Google and Unity for ads and crash reports is kept and deleted under their own policies, linked above, which also explain how to ask them to delete it.
