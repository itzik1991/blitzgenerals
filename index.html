Blitz Generals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Blitz Generals is a single-player strategy game played against the computer. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat. Your settings and your last army are saved only on your own device.

Crash and performance reports

The game is built with the Unity engine. To help fix crashes and performance problems, the Unity engine may automatically send crash reports and technical diagnostic data to Unity Technologies. This data describes the device and the error (for example the device model, operating system version and the error details) and may include a device identifier generated for the app. It does not include your name, contacts, location or files. This data is sent over an encrypted connection and is not used for advertising. Unity's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
